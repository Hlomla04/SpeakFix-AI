System Flow — The Closed-Loop Lifecycle
From the moment a user speaks to the moment a ticket is closed, every step is recorded, verified, and reversible. A ticket is only "Resolved" when the original reporter confirms the fix.

Loop back to Phase 3 — Maintenance works again
↻ The closed loop
After the ticket is reopened, the technician repairs the issue again, submits a new resolution with evidence, and the ticket returns to AWAITING_VERIFICATION. SpeakFix asks the reporter again. The loop repeats until the reporter confirms — only then is the ticket closed as RESOLVED — USER CONFIRMED.

A technician submitting a resolution does not close the ticket. The ticket returns to AWAITING_VERIFICATION, and only the original reporter's confirmation can transition it to RESOLVED. If the reporter says the problem persists, the ticket is reopened and the loop runs again. SpeakFix closes the loop.
